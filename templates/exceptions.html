🧠 Exception → Decision → Resolution

🔴 Damaged Item

Decision: Remove damaged quantity from usable stock.

Resolution: Record the damaged item and prevent it from being allocated.

🟠 Missing Item

Decision: Stop fulfillment until the item is located.

Resolution: Search warehouse location or reallocate stock from another location.

🟡 Stock Shortage

Decision: Allocate available quantity based on order priority.

Resolution: Recommend replenishment or partial fulfillment.

When an exception occurs, the system should identify the problem, evaluate its impact, recommend an action and track the resolution.
