🔄 Packing Workflow

1️⃣ Picked

Products have been successfully collected from the warehouse.

2️⃣ Pack

Products are packed using suitable packaging materials.

3️⃣ Verify

Product quantity and order details are verified.

4️⃣ Quality Check

Package is checked for damage, missing items and correctness.

5️⃣ Approve

Approved orders are moved to dispatch.

6️⃣ Dispatch

Final package is handed over for shipment.

⚠️ Packing Exception Rules

📦 Missing Item

Stop packing and report the missing product for investigation.

⚠️ Damaged Item

Separate the damaged product and create an exception record.

🔍 Wrong Item

Do not dispatch. Return the item to picking for correction.
