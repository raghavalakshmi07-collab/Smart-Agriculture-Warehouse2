📍 Picking Process

1️⃣ Prioritize

Urgent and high-priority orders are processed first.

2️⃣ Locate

Warehouse staff identify the product storage location.

3️⃣ Pick

Required quantities are picked from available stock.

4️⃣ Verify

Picked products are checked against the order.

5️⃣ Complete

Once picking is complete, the order moves to packing.

6️⃣ Track

Order status is updated throughout the workflow.

🤖 Smart Picking Decision

Decision Rule:

Process Urgent orders first, followed by High, Medium and Low priority orders.
