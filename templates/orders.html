🧠 Smart Order Prioritization

The warehouse uses priority levels to decide which orders should be processed first.

🔴 Urgent

Orders requiring immediate attention. These are processed before other orders.

🟠 High

Important orders that should be processed before normal priority orders.

🟡 Medium

Normal operational orders handled after urgent and high-priority orders.

🟢 Low

Orders that can be processed when higher priority work is completed.
